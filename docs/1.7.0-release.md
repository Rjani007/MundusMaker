# MundusMaker 1.7.0 — characters and organization

This release makes creating and advancing characters a guided workflow, gives
each supported system its own printable sheet, and lets DMs organize their
world into categories such as Gods without editing configuration files.

## Create a character

Open **Create a character** from the dashboard or Characters page. The D&D
2024 builder now provides:

- A step navigator, completion checks and a live character summary.
- Allowed-source switches and searchable, source-labelled browsers for the
  bundled SRD and local 5etools packs, with descriptions beside choices.
- Class, planned subclass, species, background, origin feat and Fighting Style
  choices; skill proficiencies, expertise and languages.
- Standard array, recommended assignments, 27-point buy, 4d6 drop-lowest rolls,
  and manual scores. Background bonuses are checked separately.
- Separate class and background equipment options, editable inventory and
  gold, worn armor and shield selection, and calculated AC and weapon attacks.
- Class spell quotas, a separate Wizard spellbook, and Magic Initiate spells.
- A review step with personality, alignment and backstory.

Drafts live in session storage per world and system. Leaving and returning in
the same tab resumes the draft. An older per-system draft is adopted into the
current world. No character entry is written before Finish; a failed save
retries the same entry rather than creating duplicates.

Bundled options win name clashes; among imported copies, the newest import
takes precedence. Imported subclasses can attach to bundled
classes. Packs carry class progression tables, feat ability increases and
fixed subclass spell grants through the server's existing whitelist. Older
packs may need re-importing or downloading again. Missing spell progression
produces an explanation rather than invented spell slots.

## Advance after play

**Level up…** opens a preview-and-apply workflow on a D&D sheet. Each use
advances one level and records its choices in the advancement history.

- Average or rolled HP, with retroactive Constitution increases and applicable
  Dwarf/Tough bonuses; temporary HP is retained.
- Subclass selection at level 3, with planned choices kept for that level.
- Ability Score Improvement or an eligible feat, including feat ability
  increases, level/ability prerequisite checks and additional-choice notes.
- Expertise, Barbarian/Lore Bard skill gains, Fighting Styles, and Magic
  Initiate's separate spell selections.
- Updated proficiency, generated weapon attacks, automatic AC and spell DCs.
  AC and attack numbers edited manually remain manual during advancement.
- Cantrips, prepared spells, Wizard spellbook additions, fixed class/subclass
  spell grants, and slots from each class's actual progression table. Free
  prepared spells are kept outside the normal preparation quota.
- Spent slots remain spent, including when Pact Magic's slot level rises.

The builder targets **single-class D&D 2024 characters**. It is not a complete
automation of every feature or imported rule. Choices such as invocations,
metamagic, ancestry effects, conditional bonuses and unusual pack mechanics
can be recorded as feature notes and adjusted in the existing sheet editor.
Multiclassing is not calculated. V5 XP advancement and CoC development remain
available in their existing editors.

## Print character sheets

Each supported sheet has standalone **Player sheet PDF** and **GM sheet PDF**
actions, with A4 and US Letter paper choices. These are original system layouts,
not reproductions of official publisher artwork or fillable publisher forms.

| System | Printable structure |
| --- | --- |
| D&D 2024 | Abilities/modifiers, saves, skills/expertise, passive perception, AC/initiative/speed, HP, hit dice/death saves, attacks, equipment, traits and spellcasting |
| Vampire V5 | Physical/social/mental attribute and skill columns, dots, health/willpower damage tracks, hunger, humanity, disciplines and character details |
| Call of Cthulhu 7e | Characteristics and half/fifth thresholds, HP/MP/SAN/luck, combat, skills and investigator details |

Descriptions and large lists continue onto additional pages. D&D output
includes the SRD attribution. World PDFs append dedicated character sheets by
default; **Compact text** preserves the shorter original rendering.

The same `gmOnly` and block `secret` flags govern standalone and world exports.
A player-mode call cannot request GM content. Secret continuations repeat the
GM banner. Session tracker content remains excluded from both editions.

## Organize the world

**Categories** is available from the sidebar and dashboard. DMs can create,
rename and reorder categories; set singular/plural names, color and suggested
facts; and use them immediately in entry creation and the character builder.
Category keys stay stable when display names change.

The sidebar has a category jump menu and a connected-entries section for the
current entry. Category pages provide a switcher, search across names/tags/
facts, a relationship filter, and grouping by connections or tags. Connected
groups use the existing relationship graph, including shared faction hubs.
Secret edges and hidden entries cannot supply player-visible group labels.

## Ready-written DM Screen references

**Browse ready-written rules** offers 36 references, stored locally with the
app. Choose a system and topic, search, read a preview, then pin a reference:

- **D&D 2024:** all fifteen conditions as individual cards; abilities and
  eighteen skills; DCs/proficiency; turns/actions; attacks/cover; spellcasting
  and Concentration; damage/death saves; rests; travel/light/hazards.
- **Vampire V5:** dice pools, Hunger/Rouse checks, messy criticals/bestial
  failures, Willpower rerolls, conflict/damage and attributes/skills.
- **Call of Cthulhu 7e:** percentile/opposed tests, bonus/penalty/Luck dice,
  combat/defense, wounds/healing, Sanity, development and a Keeper checklist.

Pinning creates an ordinary GM-only Note with source attribution. Browsing
writes nothing. Edited copies are preserved and reused on re-pin, including
after a failed-save retry. Copies travel in world backups; the screen still
stores only entry IDs. Player preview and player exports exclude these notes
through the existing secrecy flags. D&D reminders adapt the attributed SRD
5.2.1; V5 and CoC reminders are original mechanical summaries, not copied book
prose or power descriptions. The system creation JSON files are unchanged.

## One download for Windows, Mac and Linux

**MundusMaker-1.7.0.zip** contains all five checksum-verified Node runtimes:
Windows x64, macOS Apple silicon/Intel, Linux x64/ARM64. The familiar `.bat`,
`.sh` and `.command` launchers are together in one folder; Mac users can open
**MundusMaker.app** directly without Terminal. Launchers select the correct
runtime automatically. The single download is larger because it contains every
supported runtime. Unix launchers and runtimes carry executable permissions
even when packaged on Windows. The ZIP includes a startup guide, application
and runtime licenses, and a checksum sidecar. Completed ZIP contents are
checked to exclude AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md,
other contributor instructions, development artifacts, real worlds and packs.

The Mac app is self-contained, including both CPU variants, so it can be moved
into Applications. Double-click it to open the browser;
the **MM** menu offers Open, startup logs and Quit without Terminal. Data and
configuration live under `~/Library/Application Support/MundusMaker`, outside
the replaceable app bundle. Existing portable worlds can be copied through
Import. Mac builds require macOS 13.5+ and remain unsigned/unnotarized tester
builds; Apple's one-time Privacy & Security approval may be needed. Linux
requires glibc 2.28+ and kernel 4.18+.

The archives and server/configuration behavior are checked on Windows. Native
Finder/menu-bar and Linux desktop launches still require testing on those
operating systems. Signing and notarization require a Mac and the publisher’s
Apple Developer credentials and are not included in these tester builds.

## Validation

- `npm run test:characters`: pure creation/advancement and category tests,
  including all twelve SRD classes advancing through all nineteen level-ups.
- Browser checks in an isolated test world: Fighter and Wizard creation,
  persisted advancement, source filters, local pack import, category creation,
  draft resume/adoption, a failed Finish followed by a successful retry,
  responsive layout and player-route gating.
- Rendered PDF checks: D&D on both paper sizes, V5 and CoC layouts, long-text
  continuation, secret banners, player/GM redaction and tracker exclusion.
- Existing storage/saving/server-safety and encounter-tracker suites remain
  part of the release gate. Development test worlds stay separate from `data/`.
- DM Screen browser checks cover pinning all three systems, editing and
  re-pinning without duplicates, failed-save retries, reload, world backups,
  narrow screens and player-mode hiding. A freshly extracted universal ZIP was
  started with its bundled runtime and checked for reference saving and the
  character builder. The universal ZIP passed an independent CRC/content/mode
  check and binary-architecture checks for all five runtimes. POSIX shell tests
  cover automatic runtime selection for both Mac and Linux CPUs, legacy
  layouts, folders with spaces, and Linux launcher execution using test
  runtimes. The bundled runtime is pinned to Node v24.20.0.

Validation on 8 September 2026 covers **49 automated tests** (16 character
and category, 16 data-safety, 7 tracker, 3 references, 7 packaging/configuration).
The original release's 46 tests passed, and the seven packaging/configuration
tests passed after the universal-package changes, including three new checks.
The browser and rendered-PDF checks above also passed. The PDF checks included extraction of hidden-content
sentinels from GM output and their absence from both player export paths.

No runtime dependencies, cloud services, accounts or AI features were added.

## Design references

The step navigation, visible choices and source controls were informed by the
public D&D Beyond [builder documentation](https://dndbeyond-support.wizards.com/hc/en-us/articles/7747202748436-Builder-Sections)
and [content-source controls](https://dndbeyond-support.wizards.com/hc/en-us/articles/7747201820948-Enabling-Content-Categories-for-your-Character-Sheet).
Rules and bundled descriptions come from the attributed, SRD-filtered local
content. No D&D Beyond character or account content was changed during this work.
