Welcome to MundusMaker

Unzip the whole folder, then start MundusMaker as shown for your system. Node.js is included. Your browser opens http://localhost:4870, and your worlds stay on your computer.

Windows

Double-click Start-MundusMaker.bat. MundusMaker runs in the background with an icon in the tray (the arrow by the clock may hide it). Closing the browser does not stop it. Install-MundusMaker.bat adds Start menu and desktop shortcuts. If something goes wrong, MundusMaker-Console.bat runs it in a window that shows every message. Windows 10 or later, x64.

Mac

Move MundusMaker.app to Applications and open it. It works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13.5 or later. The app is unsigned, so macOS may ask you to allow it once under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway (Apple's steps). The MM icon in the menu bar opens MundusMaker, shows the log and quits.

Your worlds are in ~/Library/Application Support/MundusMaker/data and the logs in ~/Library/Logs/MundusMaker. Replacing the app keeps both.

Linux

Run start-mundusmaker.sh from your file manager, or ./start-mundusmaker.sh in a terminal. It runs in the background, so you can close the terminal. Add --install-desktop for a menu entry, or --foreground to keep it in the terminal (Ctrl+C stops it). x64 or ARM64, glibc 2.28 and kernel 4.18 or later.

Quitting

Use ⏻ Quit at the top right of the app, or the tray or menu bar icon. Saves in progress finish first.

Your worlds

On Windows and Linux they are in the data folder beside the launcher. You start with an empty world called My World. To look around first, press Add the sample world on the dashboard.

Upgrading: quit the old version, unzip the new one next to the old folder and start it. It copies your worlds across and never changes the old folder. The Import page can copy worlds from an install somewhere else.

Backups and sync: see DATA-SAFETY.md. Docker or a NAS: see DOCKER.md. Anyone with the password gets full GM access.
