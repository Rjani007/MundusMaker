# MundusMaker 1.9.2

This release makes handout cards straight from the Library, exports handouts
as images, imports character sheet PDFs from D&D Beyond, and fixes two
security problems. All 1.9.1 features remain included, and no map changes:
the map generators were not touched.

## Handout cards from the Library

- **Cards from the library…** on the Handouts page opens a picker for
  monsters, spells and items, with search, filters and several picks at once.
  Every Library row also gains a **◇ Card** button. One new card opens in the
  designer; several join the packet, ready to export as images.
- Cards come from the bundled SRD and from your imported 5etools packs. The
  text goes into the card's own entry, where you can edit it like any other
  handout, and making the same row again reuses its card.
- SRD cards carry the CC-BY credit line. Monster cards show players only the
  name and kind; AC, hit points, speed and CR stay in a secret block.
- A card that is too full tries smaller type, then the larger 100 × 150 mm
  card, so the player edition fits one card.

## Handouts as images

- **Export image** and **Export packet images** save each page as a PNG named
  after the handout's title and edition (`-p1`, `-p2` for longer handouts).
  Ask for one ZIP if you want them together; the phone app always makes one.
- An item card exports as just the card, not a mostly blank A4 page. Sheets
  of cards with cutting guides stay in the PDF.

## Import a D&D character sheet PDF

- The Import page has a new **D&D character sheet PDF (D&D Beyond)** card. It
  reads the PDF from D&D Beyond's "Export to PDF" (checked against a real
  2024-rules character), the free 2024 D&D character sheet, and other fillable
  sheets by their field names.
- You see a preview first, and nothing is saved until you press Import. If an
  entry already has that name, MundusMaker asks before going on.
- Nothing is invented. Values the PDF doesn't have keep their defaults and are
  listed. Where MundusMaker works out a number differently (a save, a skill,
  passive Perception), the sheet's own value goes into the notes. A
  multiclass character arrives with their first class at their total level,
  with a warning, because the character builder follows one class.
- A PDF without form fields (flattened, printed or scanned) gets a plain
  explanation instead of an empty character.
- Attacks can now show a save DC as written ("DC 15") instead of "+0 to hit".

## Fixes

- **Character sheets and encounters show every stored value as text.**
  Until now a crafted entry could carry HTML in a number field (AC, hit
  points, a rating, an encounter's party size), and that HTML ran as a script
  when the entry opened. Such an entry could arrive in a backup someone gave
  you, a shared synced folder or a copied install. A Vampire power's
  reference link now opens only web addresses.
- **Other websites can no longer show MundusMaker inside a frame.** A page
  could otherwise have laid the app under something you click and used your
  clicks in it.
- **SRD 5.2 equipment**: arcane foci (crystal, orb, rod, wand) are gear rather
  than shields, and armor is marked light, medium or heavy.

## Verification

`tests/sheet-markup.test.js` puts HTML into every sheet and encounter value
and checks that it comes out as text; `tests/request-guard.test.js` checks
the framing headers. `tests/handouts.test.js` covers library cards, card
fitting, image sizes and file names, and `tests/dndpdfimport.test.js` builds
every kind of sheet PDF itself and reads it back. The full suite (195 tests)
and the phone runtime check pass, and the map hash gate matches all 60
baselines in Chrome 155. In Edge 156, installed this week, eight town and
region maps differ by a few anti-aliased pixels along the edges of rotated
shapes, such as a farm field's outline or a building's shadow. Nothing moves,
and the unchanged 1.9.1 code shows the same eight, so those baselines were not
re-recorded.

One **MundusMaker-1.9.2.zip** still covers Windows, Apple silicon/Intel Macs
and Linux x64/ARM64. The Android tester app is 1.9.2 as well.
