# MundusMaker 1.9.1

This release adds two map generators: a **Terrain world map** in the style of
mapgen4, with optional erosion, and a **Modern city map** whose streets follow
a tensor field. Both are new choices in the new-map menu. Every map made
before keeps exactly the look it had: all 60 render baselines still match.
All 1.9.0 features remain included.

Both generators were written for MundusMaker from published methods. No code
from those projects is included, and Settings → About credits them.

## Terrain world maps

Choose **Terrain world map (mapgen4-style)** for a new world map, or set Style
to Terrain on an existing one. The approach follows Amit Patel's mapgen4 (Red
Blob Games): a triangle mesh over blue-noise points, a painted "constraint"
that decides land, hills and mountains, rain carried by the wind, and rivers
that drain to the sea.

- **Mountains** form ranges that wander across the land and throw off spurs.
  High ground you have not painted stays an upland cut by its ranges.
- **Brushes**: besides Raise and Lower, Terrain maps have Mountains, Hills,
  Lowland and Sea brushes. Each paints the land toward that kind of ground, so
  going over a spot again settles it instead of piling it up. The Mountains
  brush brings up peaks wherever it goes; painting Hills over them wears them
  down.
- **Climate**: the wind's rain falls on the windward slopes and leaves a rain
  shadow behind the ranges, and every coast gets moisture from the sea, so dry
  country lies inland and behind mountains rather than across a whole half of
  the map. The Rainfall slider makes the world wetter or drier.
- **Sea**: shallow shelves follow every coast before the drop to open ocean,
  with two depth lines drawn along the shore.
- **Rivers and lakes**: rivers widen as they gather water. Deep basins that a
  river keeps full become lakes (at most six), and the river carries on from
  the lake's outlet.
- **Erosion** (optional): *Rain & sediment* runs water droplets over the land
  so they cut gullies and leave silt in the valleys, then lets over-steep
  slopes slide into scree; silted flats show greener and worn slopes rockier.
  *River networks* grows branching rivers up from the coast and carves valleys
  along them, leaving ridges between. These are the procedural methods of
  Daniel Andrino's "Terrain Erosion 3 Ways".
- Nations, settlements, map keys, site edits, region drill-downs and town maps
  work as they do on Atlas maps. Region maps opened from a Terrain world
  inherit its terrain, towns, rivers and lakes.
- Painting stays responsive: the map redraws once per frame at half size while
  you drag, and settlements, lakes and erosion settle when you let go.
  Coastlines are traced at full resolution, so they stay smooth in 2× and 4×
  exports.

## Modern city maps

Choose **Modern city map (tensor-field streets)**, or set Layout to Metro on a
modern town. Streets follow the method of Chen, Esch, Wonka, Müller and Zhang
("Interactive Procedural Street Modeling", 2008), which Keir's MapGenerator
made popular: grids that meet at angles, streets that bend along the shore and
the river, and curving suburbs.

- Main avenues, collectors and local streets, each spaced to suit its class.
  Avenues carry on out of town to the map's edge as highways.
- A drive runs along the sea front and a road down each river bank, with a
  beach or green bank between the road and the water. Bridges cross rivers
  square-on.
- A railway crosses the city past downtown, with a station and platform. Main
  roads cross the tracks and side streets end at them.
- One or two big parks near the centre, each with a pond and footpaths, plus
  squares and neighbourhood parks.
- City blocks split into lots, buildings suited to each district, landmarks
  to key (City Hall, the station, a hospital, schools, a marina…), and
  storeys that cast shadows: towers downtown, houses at the edge.
- Day and night themes, GM renames for streets and landmarks, and Populate
  work as on other modern towns. "Surprise me" never picks Metro, so existing
  modern towns keep their layout.

## Test builds

Maps made with the 1.9.0 mapgen test builds use these generators in earlier
forms. Opened in 1.9.1, they are generated again from their seeds with the
final rules, so their mountains, lakes, streets and railway will differ.
Painted strokes, markers, labels and other hand edits stay where they were put.

## Verification

`tests/terrain-metro.test.js` covers the triangulation, determinism, live
painting against a rebuild, brushes, lakes, drainage in every erosion mode,
coastal shelves and coastal rain, uplands, the rasterizer, and for cities:
buildings off the streets and out of the water, shore roads, big parks,
storeys, railways that cross rivers square-on, streets with no loose scraps,
and GM renames. The full suite (180 tests) and the map hash gate pass. No
render baselines were recorded for the two new generators yet.

One **MundusMaker-1.9.1.zip** still covers Windows, Apple silicon/Intel Macs
and Linux x64/ARM64.
