MundusMaker

A worldbuilding wiki for game masters. It runs on your own computer. No account, no subscription.

  • Secrets stay secret. Lock any paragraph, link or map pin, and player exports leave it out.
  • Maps at every scale: world, region, town, building and dungeon.
  • Characters for D&D 2024, Vampire: The Masquerade 5th Edition and Call of Cthulhu 7th Edition.
  • Handouts and a player wiki for your table.
