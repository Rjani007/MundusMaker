MundusMaker

A worldbuilding wiki for game masters. Use it in your browser with a free account, or download it and run it on your own computer with no account at all.

  • Secrets stay secret. Lock any paragraph, link or map pin, and player exports leave it out.
  • Maps at every scale: world, region, town, building and dungeon.
  • Characters for D&D 2024, Vampire: The Masquerade 5th Edition and Call of Cthulhu 7th Edition.
  • Handouts and a player wiki for your table.
Open MundusMaker in your browser Download for your computer Share a player wiki

In the browser, your worlds are kept in that browser and in your account, so they open on any computer or phone you sign in on. The download keeps them in a folder on your computer, and nothing goes online.
